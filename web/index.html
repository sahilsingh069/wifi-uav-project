Wi-Fi / cellular mobility prediction + multi-agent RL

RF-Guided UAV Coverage

Drones can't see the people on the ground. They only receive the users' Wi-Fi and cellular signals. An LSTM-Transformer turns those signals into estimated positions, and the drones move to keep as many users as possible inside their coverage.

    How it works
    1. Simulate signals. Users walk randomly (Gauss-Markov). For each step, Wi-Fi and cellular signal strength and CSI are generated with 3GPP TR 38.901 channel models.
    2. Predict positions. An LSTM-Transformer reads the last 10 signal measurements and estimates where each user will be 5 steps later.
    3. Coordinate drones. MADDPG trains one actor per UAV with a shared critic. Altitude matters: a higher drone has a wider beam footprint but a weaker link.
    4. Compare honestly. Every policy flies the same held-out episodes, and its score is the average coverage over every step.

    All data is simulated. This is a research prototype, not a real flight controller.
